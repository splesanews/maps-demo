This is the most stylish hotel in the Yorkshire Dales

Swish Grantley Hall offers more indulgence than you could hope to cram into one weekend and has added a village pub to its Michelin-starred food and art-covered walls

Laura Jackson
Monday March 09 2026, 5.00am GMT, The Times

As Restoration-era stately piles go, Grantley Hall's low-rise, two-storey exterior near Ripon in the east of the Yorkshire Dales National Park is comparatively understated, but do not be taken in by the subtlety. A £70 million refurb by the owner Valeria Sykes in 2019 restored the 1680s mansion from a council-owned education college into a grand hotel that is a lesson in how to do five-star service and interiors properly. There are 47 lavish bedrooms and suites to kick back in, deep relaxation in the Three Graces Spa and five restaurants and three bars on site, including Shaun Rankin's Michelin-starred spot. The hotel's newest addition, however, the Grantley Arms village pub, a five-minute drive away, is a traditional slice of Yorkshire hospitality complete with tiddlywinks and Timothy Taylor's Landlord on draught.

Rooms and suites

Score 9/10 Bedrooms and suites are split between two buildings, the old hall and the modern Fountains extension, taking inspiration from the ancient abbey nearby. Regardless of whether you go old or modern, Cosy room or Signature suite, all are finished to the same standard featuring marble bathrooms with Bulgari smellies and underfloor heating, TVs hidden behind artworks and house-made damson gin. Plush soft furnishings from cushions to sofas are in creams and light browns with dark-wood trims, paired with mid-century-style lighting. Comfort factors are heightened thanks to a robe menu; go fluffy, bamboo or waffle. Each bedroom has something distinctive about it, for example Room 20 in the Executive category has a copper bath by the window and Suite 38 comes with a shower that converts to a private sauna. There are two sumptuous suites for the one percenters, the Presidential Suite on the Fountains side houses a grand piano and a bar, while the Royal Suite in the historic wing has an Emperor bed on the mezzanine floor.

Food and drink

Score 10/10 Expect a weekend at Grantley to feel like a human v food challenge. Top billing goes to Shaun Rankin's restaurant, which has retained its Michelin star for 2026, but the standard everywhere else is exceptional too. Bar & Restaurant EightyEight is in a separate building overlooking the Japanese garden and has an Asian theme. Start with a Haiku cocktail in the bar upstairs then head downstairs to the restaurant for delicate lobster and prawn dumplings, beef fillet and phenomenal sides including a KFC, Korean fried cauliflower.

Details are what make Grantley worth the room rates. Order a full English during breakfast at Fletchers not just for the sausage and black pudding but the bulbous deep spoon designed for scooping baked beans. There is humour too. Wooden owls roost on perches in Fletchers, while in the Orchard, a tented restaurant and Moët & Chandon bar serving fine tapas and larger sharing plates, has a vintage Fiat 500 parked by the entrance.

Opened in summer 2025, the Grantley Arms is the hotel's pub a 20-minute walk away in Grantley village (it's uphill so do ask for a lift at reception). In the warm-toned 17th-century sandstone building the food here goes beyond gastropub grub, starters such as scallop risotto and mains of venison with haggis balls sit on the menu next to brick-sized burgers and fish and chips. The family have kept the trad boozer vibes with a huge fireplace, wooden stools and original flagstone floors.

Service everywhere is slick and devoid of five-star stuffiness, expect chatty and mostly local Yorkshire staff who cannot do enough for you.

What else is there?

The Three Graces Spa is a big draw, in a purpose-built Roman-style complex complete with decorative obelisks and classical statues, an 18m swimming pool and heated indoor-outdoor hydrotherapy pool. Brave the cold in the sub-zero snow room that's handily between the sauna and steam room. The treatment menu has offerings from the Harley Street brand 111Skin (its two-hour facial starts with a footbath, back massage and facial) and the Spanish skincare specialists Natura Bissé, with full body massages to manicures available.

Across from the spa, the Elite gym, with the latest LifeFitness cardio and weights machines, would please a professional athlete. Outside there are 38 acres of grounds, including a charming Japanese garden and walks by the River Skell.

Motorsports are beloved here and in addition to the indoor simulator there are supercars from Lamborghinis to Porsches to browse, lining the drive. Oh, and should you be arriving by chopper there is a helipad a few minutes from the front door too.

Where is it?

Score 9/10 If you are not coming by car (or helicopter), the nearest stations are Thirsk (40-minutes drive northeast) or Harrogate (30-minutes southeast). Grantley Hall is a ten-minute drive from Ripon and its cathedral and Fountains Abbey. For hikers the Yorkshire Dales to the west and North York Moors to the east are calling.
